# Miaob — Your Chinese Reading Companion

## Summary

Browse Chinese web pages and automatically discover idioms, famous quotes, and highlights. Click to view explanations, origins, and context — then save and share the best parts of any article.

## Discover the gems in Chinese articles

Miaob is a Chrome extension for reading Chinese content. Open an article, and it will find the idioms, classic quotes, xiehouyu (Chinese riddles), and noteworthy expressions — gently highlighting them in the original text.

See an unfamiliar idiom? Look up its meaning and origin instantly. Find a beautifully written sentence? Save, copy, or share it with one click.

## What you can do with Miaob

**Discover idioms and allusions**
While reading news, novels, or social media posts, idioms are automatically highlighted. Hover or click to learn their meanings, origins, and related information.

**Find the highlights in articles**
Miaob identifies insightful, powerful, and memorable sentences — and points out rhetorical devices like parallelism, contrast, and rhetorical questions.

**Generate an article highlight share**
Click "Share discoveries" to generate a shareable link. Friends don't need the extension to see the article's best expressions and original link.

**Save what you love**
Collect your favorite idioms and quotes in your Miaoben notebook, and revisit them anytime.

**Bilingual interface**
The UI automatically switches between Chinese and English to match your browser language — great for international readers.

## Perfect for

- Reading news, novels, and social media articles
- Learning idioms, allusions, and classic expressions
- Finding better phrasing for your own writing
- Sharing the best parts of an article with friends
- Reading Chinese articles more attentively

## Simple, quiet, unobtrusive

Miaob doesn't cover the article or bombard you with popups. It makes light annotations on the page — open details only when you want to. New content loaded by scrolling is also analyzed automatically.

## Privacy

When checking is enabled, the page text is sent to the Miaob server for real-time analysis. Content you actively share or provide feedback on is saved according to each feature. We don't collect browsing history, bookmarks, or passwords, and we never sell personal information.

For details: https://miaob.net/privacy
